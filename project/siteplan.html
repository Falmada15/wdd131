Website Planning Document

Site Name

⚽ MESSI: THE LAST DANCE

Lionel Messi: The Last Dance

This name represents what could be Lionel Messi's final FIFA World Cup appearance in 2026. The site celebrates his journey, achievements, legacy, and impact on football fans around the world.

Site Purpose

This website celebrates Lionel Messi's journey with Argentina and explores what could be his final FIFA World Cup appearance in 2026. It highlights his legacy, achievements, memorable moments, statistics, and the impact he has had on football fans worldwide. The website will also provide information about Argentina's World Cup journey, historic matches, images, and interactive content created with JavaScript.

Scenarios

  • What are Lionel Messi's most memorable moments with Argentina?
  • How did Messi overcome challenges throughout his international career?
  • What legacy will Messi leave for future generations of football players?
  • Why is the 2026 FIFA World Cup so significant for Messi's fans?

Color Schema

Sky Blue (#6CB4EE)

Used for the header, navigation, accents, and elements inspired by Argentina's national colors.

Dark Blue (#003366)

Used for headings, footer, highlighted sections, and important content.

Typography

Playfair Display – Headings, section titles, and featured content.

Roboto – Body text, navigation, and general content.
